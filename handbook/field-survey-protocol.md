# Field survey protocol (1 m² plots)

Purpose: turn "I felt there were plenty" into comparable evidence, and record true absences.

1. Choose a forest stand you can reach legally. Note GPS accuracy; wait for under 20 m if you can.
2. Walk a straight line. Every 10 m or more, place a 1 m × 1 m frame (or a string square) on the ground. Do 5 plots.
3. In each plot count **ripe** and **unripe** berries of the target species; count flowers in early season. Zero is a real answer.
4. Enter the plot count and the totals in the app (Field mode → "I counted standardised 1 m² plots").
5. Take one photo of the ground layer from above (optional, helps review).
6. Do not survey only the places you already know are good. Mixed sites make the data useful.

What counts as an **absence** for training: a 1 m² plot survey with zero berries, or a visit of at least 10 minutes that found nothing.
"Didn't look properly" and unvisited places are **never** absences. Repeat visits by one person to one stand in one season count once.
